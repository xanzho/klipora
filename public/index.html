Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using KlipOra.

How do I download videos using KlipOra? ＋

Copy the video link from TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, or X (Twitter). Paste it into KlipOra's search bar above and tap "Fetch Media". Select MP4 Video or MP3 Audio to start the instant download.

Is KlipOra free to use on mobile devices? ＋

Yes, KlipOra is 100% free across all iPhones, iPads, Android smartphones, laptops, and desktop computers.

Are downloaded files stored on KlipOra servers? ＋

No. KlipOra does not host, save, or log any media files on its servers. All media is fetched and streamed straight from third-party social media CDNs.
